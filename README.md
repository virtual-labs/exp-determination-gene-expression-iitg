### About the Experiment

This experiment covers the isolation of mRNA from mammalian cells and its separation on a denaturing agarose gel that prevents secondary structure formation.
The size-separated RNA is transferred and immobilised onto a nitrocellulose membrane, and the transfer is verified on a UV transilluminator.
The membrane is hybridised with a labelled cDNA probe complementary to the transcript of interest and then washed to remove the excess probe.
The hybridised RNA is visualised on an autoradiogram to determine the expression level of a particular gene.
